About this dashboard

This dashboard was built for Life Ready Facilitated Care team members by Iberian Pacific Financial Services Inc., your group plan advisor. It brings together the pieces of your group plan that used to live in separate emails and logins into one place.

What's included

  • A direct link to your group investment account provider
  • A direct link to your group insurance provider
  • Plain-English education on budgeting, debt, credit, investing, and retirement

Your Iberian Pacific advisor manages the relationship with both providers on Life Ready's behalf, and is available any time you have a question about your plan.
