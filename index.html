Welcome to the Life Ready Learning Hub

The Life Ready Learning Hub is a free educational resource from Leigh Grant Financial designed to help you feel more confident about everyday money decisions. Explore practical, plain-English guidance on budgeting, debt, credit, savings, protection, and planning ahead.

What you'll find here

  • Friendly guides on budgeting, debt, credit, saving, protection, and planning ahead
  • Short videos (coming soon) and practical examples to make key ideas easier to understand
  • A free spending plan template to help you get organized and take the next step

This hub is here to educate and support. The information provided is general in nature and not personalized financial advice. Ready for a more personal conversation about your financial goals? We're here for you.

Our Approach

Clear guidance, thoughtful protection, and education to help you move forward with greater confidence.

Trusted connections, when you need them. Our role is to help make life's decisions a little easier. When you need support outside our area of expertise, we're happy to connect you with trusted professionals we've worked with and feel comfortable referring.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Life Ready Learning Hub?

The Life Ready Learning Hub is a free financial education website with articles, videos, and templates on budgeting, debt, credit, investing, insurance, and retirement planning in Canada. It was created by Leigh Grant Financial for the Life Ready Facilitated Care community and is open to everyone.

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, everything is free and there is no account or sign-up. You can read every article, view the spending plan template, and watch videos once they launch, without providing any personal information.

Is this financial advice?

No. All content is general education to help you understand how money topics work. It does not consider your personal situation, so speak with a qualified financial professional before making decisions about your own finances.

Can I access my group benefits or investment account here?

No. The Life Ready Learning Hub is an education-only resource and does not connect to any benefits, insurance, or investment account. For questions about an existing account or policy, contact your plan provider directly.

Where can I get the free budget template?

The free Complete Financial Spending Plan is on the Templates page. It is a view-only Google Sheet with tabs for a monthly spending plan, financial snapshot, dashboard, goals tracker, accounts, and tax savings. You can request your own editable copy directly in Google Sheets.
