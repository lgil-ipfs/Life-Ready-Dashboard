FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Life Ready Learning Hub?

The Life Ready Learning Hub is a free financial education website with articles, videos, and templates on budgeting, debt, credit, investing, insurance, and retirement planning in Canada. It was created by Iberian Pacific Financial Services Inc. for the Life Ready Facilitated Care community and is open to everyone.

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, everything is free and there is no account or sign-up. You can read every article, watch every video, and view the spending plan template without providing any personal information.

Is this financial advice?

No. All content is general education to help you understand how money topics work. It does not consider your personal situation, so speak with a qualified financial professional before making decisions about your own finances.

Can I access my group benefits or investment account here?

No. The Life Ready Learning Hub is an education-only resource and does not connect to any benefits, insurance, or investment account. For questions about an existing account or policy, contact your plan provider directly.

Where can I get the free budget template?

The free Complete Financial Spending Plan is on the Templates page. It is a view-only Google Sheet with tabs for a monthly spending plan, financial snapshot, dashboard, goals tracker, accounts, and tax savings. You can request your own editable copy directly in Google Sheets.
