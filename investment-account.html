Group Investment Account

Your Life Ready group plan includes a group investment account (such as a Group RRSP) managed by your plan provider. Use the link below to sign in and view your account.

What you can do in your account

  • View your current balance and investment performance
  • See your and Life Ready's contributions
  • Update your beneficiary and personal information
  • Change your investment fund selections
  • Download statements and tax slips

First time signing in?

You'll need the plan provider's member ID from your enrollment package. If you haven't received your enrollment details, contact your Iberian Pacific group plan advisor and we'll help you get set up.

Questions about contributions or investment options

Your Iberian Pacific advisor can walk you through your fund lineup, contribution rates, and how to make changes — reach out any time using the button below.

Sign in to your account

You'll be redirected to your plan provider's secure member portal.
