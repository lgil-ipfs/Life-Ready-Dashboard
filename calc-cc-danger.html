Credit Card Minimum Payment Calculator

See the real cost of only paying the minimum on a credit card, and how much faster you get out of debt with even a small extra payment.

Your credit card details

How minimum payments usually work in Canada: most cards set the minimum at a small percentage of your balance (often around 2% to 3%) or a fixed floor like $10, whichever is greater. This calculator uses 2% or $10. Check your statement for your card's exact formula.

The minimum payment trap

Minimum payments shrink as your balance shrinks, so a big share of every payment goes to interest and the balance barely moves. Adding even $25 to $50 a month on top of the minimum can cut years off your payoff and save hundreds or thousands of dollars in interest.
