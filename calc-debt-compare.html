      Which strategy should you choose?

      Debt avalanche: pay the debt with the highest interest rate first while making minimum payments on everything else. When it's cleared, roll its payment into the next-highest rate. This keeps total interest as low as possible.

      Debt snowball: pay the smallest balance first. You clear accounts sooner, and those early wins help many people stay motivated. The trade-off is usually paying a bit more interest.

      The best strategy is the one you'll stick with. Learn more in a practical plan for paying down debt.
