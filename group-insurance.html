Group Insurance

Your Life Ready group plan includes group insurance coverage — such as health, dental, and life benefits — managed by your insurance provider. Use the link below to sign in and manage your coverage.

What you can do in the member portal

  • View your health, dental, and life coverage details
  • Submit and track claims
  • Find and download your benefits booklet
  • Order or print your benefits card
  • Update your dependents and beneficiary information

Submitting a claim

Most claims can be submitted directly through the provider's portal or mobile app. Keep your receipts and any required forms handy before you start.

Questions about your coverage

If you're not sure what's covered, or need help understanding your benefits, your Iberian Pacific group plan advisor can help — reach out any time using the button below.
